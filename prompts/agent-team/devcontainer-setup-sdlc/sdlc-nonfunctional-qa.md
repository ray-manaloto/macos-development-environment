# SDLC Non-Functional QA Prompt

Objective: verify reliability and operational robustness.

Requirements:
- Evaluate reproducibility, determinism, and rerun behavior.
- Evaluate tool/runtime prerequisites and failure handling.
- Evaluate throughput/concurrency risks and logging quality.
- Cover idempotence and amd64 vs arm64 platform implications explicitly.
- Provide hard-gate recommendations.
- End with an explicit QA sign-off statement.
